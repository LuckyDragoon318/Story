Seastone is a rare nonfoaliated metamorphic rock. It forms underwater where there is equally minimal pressure from all directions, and also high heat.
## Appearance
Seastone is a bright blue, almost white when viewed straight on, but when viewed from an angle, it can appear green or pink, or a mix of the two
## Formation
Seastone forms similarly to other nonfoaliated metamorphic rock, under water with, low equal pressure from all directions, and high heat.
