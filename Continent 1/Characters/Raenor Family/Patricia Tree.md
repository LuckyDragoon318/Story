Patricia is a musician, an exceptional one even. She sings and plays guitar.

She is the lead singer and rhythm guitar in her band [[Stand Up Girl]], with drummer [[Andra Hugo]] (Andy Hurley), bassist [[Petal Water]] (Pete Wentz), and guitarist [[Jessica Twoham]] (Joe Trohman)

Mother of [[Emanuel Raenor]], grandmother of [[Kyra Raenor]]
Wife of [[Simon Tree]]
