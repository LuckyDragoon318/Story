## Geography
Being the largest continent, the geography is extremely varied. 

### Unique features
there is a coastal mountain range in the North-West edge

on the east coast a large, active volcano. It contains the forge where the [[Seastone Weapons]] were forged. 

the center of the northern territory, just south of the coast, is a large, extremely deep, and extremely salty lake (roughly 150,000 PPM NaCl, ocean water is roughly 35,000 PPM, so about 4.3 times more salt than the ocean), the lake drains through a small river that flows north into the ocean, however the water line does not ever visibly change, regardless of a monsoon or drought, the water level never changes. Beneath the lake is an abandoned city, preserved by the salt it once mined. however, once the mine flooded, the company pulled out, and the town was abandoned.
