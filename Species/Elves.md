Elves are a magical species, they are distant "cousins" to the [[Shapeshifters]]. Not all of them, but many elves are born with a level of inherent magical abilities. Elves, in contrast to [[Humans]], who tend to fear magic, have a tendency to look down upon those who do not practice magic

There are several subtypes of Elf, with the most common being 
	[[Forest Elves]] - Specialize in [[plant magic]]
	[[Fey Elves]] - Specialize in [[trickery magic]]
	[[Dark Elves]] - Specialize in [[dark magic]]
