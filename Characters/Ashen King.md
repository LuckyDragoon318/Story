The Ashen King, Aldrath Vorne, is long dead. He was an absolute ruler, who believed with the certainty of a man who had never been wrong, that [[Aethermoor]] was in need of a single, all powerful ruler. For 30 years he waged war on all those who dared to oppose him, and he won. He was unstoppable. 

The unstoppable weapon Aldrath used was his crown. The [[Crown of Domination]] was a crown forged from Dragonsteel and infused with a fragment of the soul ripped from the god [[Malachar]]. 

The coalition that finally brought The Ashen King down had no clever trick, no divine intervention, no single hero with a legendary blade. They simply outnumbered him and were willing to die. When he fell, five of the coalition's greatest champions took the Crown to a sacred place and shattered it with a ritual that cost each of them something they could never recover. The five shards were scattered to the corners of the world.
