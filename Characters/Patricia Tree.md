Patricia is a bard, an exceptional bard even. She sings and plays guitar.
She is a humanoid platypus, she has blue skin, she has an orange beak, and webbed hands. She has a flat brown plaid tail. 

She is the lead singer and rhythm guitar in her band Stand Up Girl, with drummer [[Andra Hugo]], bassist [[Petal Water]], and guitarist [[Jessica Twoham]]
