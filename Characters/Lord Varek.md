A warlord who was born a slave in the northern wastes and clawed his way to power through sheer force of will. He's not cruel for cruelty's sake. He genuinely believes [[Aethermoor]] needs one ruler to end its endless cycle of war and famine, and he's seen enough suffering to have a point. That's what makes him dangerous. He keeps his word, respects strength, and is incapable of imagining that people might choose freedom over security.
