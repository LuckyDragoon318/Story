Kyra is a Reborn Cleric/Warlock

Family: Both parents, 1 older brother

Personality 
Friendly, not super loud, very much wants to be redeemed for doing the bidding of her patron

Kyra was an elf farmer, from the small town of Winterport. The Raenor (pronounced ray-nor) family was not overly wealthy, but they were very, very far from being poor. The Raenor family has been dedicated followers of the goddess of farming [[Folie 1]] for so many generations that even the long lived elves do not remember when their dedication to her began

On her 100th birthday Kyra left her families farm to go study in a temple of [[Folie 1]]. Her time in the temple was fairly uneventful, except for one night when Kyra went into town to get some supplies. On her way back to the temple with her shopping bags or bag of holding or whatever, BAM, she was jumped by a group of toughs! There were like 7 of them! They dragged her into a dark alley, and beat her up while demanding all of her money. Maybe 2 or 3 minutes into her beating, a blinding bright light filled the alleyway with a cloaked figure hovering infront of the light source. The thugs were disintegrated by the being's power
