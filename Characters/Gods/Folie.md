Daughter of Earth and Life

Folie (pronounced foal-ee) (her name does just mean "Madness" in French but whatever it's not pronounced the same

Goddess of agriculture

She is closest to neutral good. She does whatever she thinks is the best for the most people whenever she can, and although she doesn't want to just break whatever rules the gods have, if it will do more good than following the rule, then she will follow as best she can while still accomplishing her goal

Her holy symbol is a silver circle outline with with a set of scimitars crossed at the point where the handles turn into the blade, with the blades pointing out so that form like a circle with little horn shapes in the top, with a piece of grain in the middle of the circle

She is often depicted as a tall woman wearing robes woven from various grasses depending on the season, and a small leather pouch containing seeds for the crops that will grow well in the current weather

Summer: Japanese Blood Grass (google it, its really pretty) - the bright red represents the extreme heat in the summer, and the green is for the generally good growing that comes with summer

Spring: Lightning Bolt Switch Grass (kinda just tall green grass) - represents good growing conditions before the summer heat makes it almost too hot to work in the fields

Fall: Orange Fountain Grass (also very pretty) - represents the fading growing season, and the need to start saving food for the winter

Winter: Northwind Switch Grass (specifically when it turns tan/brown in the winter) - Represents the near impossible growing conditions and harsh weather

Depending on when, where, and who was painting or describing her, her appearance would vary wildly, usually resembling the artist

She controls the seasons and the harvest, with her mood affecting how harsh the seasons are. When she is happy, the seasons are more tame, and harvests yield more, but if she is angry with mortals then the weather might be really hot in the summer or an exceptionally cold winter. 

She is for the most part very calm, friendly, and not easily upset. She will welcome anyone to worship her, but will not discourage worship of other gods that she is on good terms with

She believes that all of nature should be respected, and that if guided well, the mortal world can find a balance between advancement and preserving nature

Her favorite animals are Honeybees, Sea Otters, and Prairie Dogs (These are all keystone species which is why she loves them, also they're cute). She also likes sheep, and canaries

Her favorite plants are cork trees and cherry trees

She was once a much more famous goddess, but as the world moves away from farming based to more industrialized she has far fewer followers, and even fewer clerics and paladins that dedicate their lives to her. At one time, Folie would have had large temples, but many of them have been left to crumble to ruin, or repurposed into other temples

Her remaining followers are mostly on continent 6, but there are some scattered throughout the rest of the world
