## More Coherent
Have you ever read a book written by someone who died? You probably have, lots of authors die, but very few of them write a book after they die. As far as I know, I'm actually the first!

I should probably explain how I’m writing this if I’m already dead. My life was very normal for a long time, I grew up with a happy family, and my famous (crazy) grandmother, [[Patricia Tree]]. But they aren’t really important now. My name is [[Kyra Raenor|Kyra]], and I am was a Wood [[Elves|Elf]] who lived just outside the small town Winterport. It was (maybe it still is, I don’t know how long I was dead) a very small town, only a couple of hundred people, nothing like [[Riverwood]]. I also come from a very religious family, for more generations than we have documented, the Raenor family have devoted themselves to Lady [[Folie|Folie]], the goddess of agriculture. 

The Raenor’s have a history of being excellent book keepers, so the eldest child in each generation becomes the head librarian in the Winterport temple to [[Continent 1/Characters/Gods 1/Folie|Folie]]. Being the oldest child, that would be my job once I reached adulthood. [[Elves]] have a very long lifespan compared to humans, we reach physical maturity only slightly slower than humans, but we are not granted adulthood until we reach 35 years of age. For a human that won’t live beyond 60 or 70, this might seem very late, but for an [[Elves|elf]] who will live 3 or four times that long, it still leaves plenty of time to be an adult. Back to how I died, when I was 35, I assumed my position as librarian in training. I was supposed to spend 5 years in training (we take the documentation of The [[Pantheon of Gods|Gods]] very seriously), but my training was cut short by the untimely murder of my father, [[Emanuel Raenor|Emanuel]] Raenor. With my training being cut short, our High Priest, [[Max Blunder]] (we call him [[Max Blunder|Mr. B]]) thought it would be a good idea to give me some extra work to keep my mind off of the death of my father. My first extra task, which I got put on instead of one of the other librarians, was to go buy more paper from town, seeing as how we don’t have the manufacturing equipment in the temple to manufacture it, though we do produce as much as we can in the temple. 

My journey into town was very boring, just a 6 mile walk in the woods, but by the time I got into town, it was starting to get a little dark, no problem though, someone will come by to light the lamps soon. Ohhh silly me, walking in the dark, alone, with a pouch of gold at my side. If you couldn’t figure it out, I got jumped. They covered my mouth, dragged me into an alley, and demanded that I hand over my gold. I obviously did so, what would you do? Fight the 5 guys who make a living beating people up? FUCK NO! 

Obviously they wouldn’t be satisfied with the story “I’m using this pouch of 100 gold to buy a bunch of sheets of paper” that sounds stupid, who would believe that? I sure wouldn’t! So they beat me up trying to get the “real” story out of me. Maybe 3 minutes into this, a brilliant green light filled the alleyway, and all five guys got yanked into the air by grape vines. The figure proceeded to, fling all five of the thugs in the back wall of the alley with what looked like nothing but a thought. The person who I was sure was Lady [[Continent 1/Characters/Gods 1/Folie|Folie]], simply floated my money back to my hand, and vanished in a green whirlpool that floated above her. Stunned, I fled into the Gnome Depot, bought my paper, and ran nearly all 6 miles back to the temple. 

Obviously I had to tell everyone about my experience once I got back to the temple.
