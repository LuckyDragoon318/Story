 A Mind Flayer that disagrees with the rest of their race, they don't believe in conquering the material plane or enslaving mortals. They are the patron for [[Kyra Raenor]]. They lurk in the shadows, hiding their appearance until their "client" has proven their loyalty. 
 Rather than being a conquerer, he is becoming the patron for adventurers to try and redeem his race
