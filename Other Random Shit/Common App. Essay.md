Where would I be without robotics? Probably not applying to college, or even taking an AP class, let alone three. In ninth grade, I barely passed several of my classes; in tenth grade, I tried out for the robotics team, with pretty low expectations, as at the time I was really struggling with self-esteem, among other things. You wouldn't believe my shock when I saw the Gmail notification that I did make the team. Fast forward to January 2025, and I learned the robotics team has "grade checks" every Thursday, meaning you need at least a C in all your classes to stay on the team. This made me panic a little because I wasn't motivated to do anything school-related. I've had a hard time admitting to myself that I'm not useless and that it's okay to be who I am. Since then, I have kept nearly all A's, been made the backup driver and operator for the 2026 FRC season, and I am expected to be the driver for the 2027 season, though tryouts won't be until February.
