### Rule Violation Penalties
Minor Foul - 5 points awarded to the opposing alliance

Major Foul - 15 points awarded to the opposing alliance

Yellow Card - *technically* no direct punishment, awarded to a team for rule violations from team member(s) or the teams robot, a second yellow card in the same competition phase will result in a red card

Red Card - issued by the HEAD REF for egregious team member or robot behavior, result is a disqualification for the current match

Disqualified - 0 points, and 0 ranking points are given to the alliance during a quals match, or 0 points during a playoff match

Disabled - robot is inoperable, and all outputs are deactivated for the remainder of the current match

Verbal warning - warning given to team by event staff or head ref

Alliance is ineligible for RP - alliance does not earn specified ranking point, regardless of other match circumstances
